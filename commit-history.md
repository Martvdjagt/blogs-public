# Commit History for Articles

The problem with AI is that it has become **so** hard to distinguish what is good from what looks good.
If I want to read articles generated by AI, then I can just ask the chat agent. 
I have a simple proposal to address this. It is not foolproof, but it at least creates another hurdle.

Save your papers, your books, your blogs in source code.
And when you are done, share your commit history.
